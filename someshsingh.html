About

I'm a product manager based in Mumbai, currently Assistant General Manager for Product at Tata Play. Most of my work sits where a large consumer business meets a digital one — figuring out what to build, why it matters to the customer, and how to get it shipped through the people who actually run the business.

I started in digital marketing in Pune and Dubai, moved into insurance at Future Generali, and then spent four years in banking product roles at YES BANK and Axis Bank, including unsecured business lending and digital banking. That run taught me how regulated products get built: slowly, with real constraints, and with a lot of stakeholders who all need to say yes.

Since 2022 I've been at Tata Play, where I work on subscription entertainment products for a customer base that spans metros and small towns, first-time smartphone users and cord-cutters alike. Designing for that spread is the most interesting part of the job.

What I care about: product vision that survives contact with a roadmap, requirements clear enough that engineering doesn't have to guess, and ideas that are worth the effort of building. Always happy to talk product.
